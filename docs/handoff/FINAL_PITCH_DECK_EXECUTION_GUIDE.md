## 🎨 3. Visual & Typography Quality Standards

- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Background: Deep Slate `#0B0F19` or Dark Navy `#0F172A`.
  - Accent / Primary CTA: Electric Blue `#3B82F6` / Cyan `#06B6D4`.
  - Positive / Recovery: Emerald Green `#10B981`.
  - Anomaly / Alert: Crimson Red `#EF4444`.
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (Inter, Montserrat, Roboto, or SF Pro). Ensure minimum 14pt body text for screen readability.
- **Contrast:** Maintain WCAG AA compliance (high contrast between white text and dark cards).

## 📄 4. PDF Export & Technical Specifications

1. **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen format (1920x1080 resolution per slide).
2. **Export Format:** High-Quality Vector PDF (Standard Presentation PDF).
