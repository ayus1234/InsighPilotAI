## 1. The 3-Minute Rapid Pitch Script (180 Seconds)

### Slide 1: Hero & Vision (0:00 – 0:20 | 20s)
> *"Judges, enterprise executives are drowning in dashboards that tell them WHAT went wrong, but leave the WHY to weeks of manual cross-silo triage. We built InsightPilot AI: an autonomous decision intelligence platform that takes an enterprise from a critical KPI anomaly to mathematically proven root causes, verifiable evidence, and simulated action levers in under 30 seconds."*

### Slide 2 & 3: The Gap & Solution (0:20 – 0:50 | 30s)
> *"Traditional BI tools are passive scorecards. Generic LLMs hallucinate numbers and lack cryptographic auditability. InsightPilot AI bridges this gap through a strict division of labor: deterministic Python analytics own 100% of the mathematical truth; an 11-node LangGraph pipeline orchestrates the investigation; and multi-model AI explains grounded facts without ever altering the numbers."*

### Slide 4 & 6: Live Scenario & Root Cause (0:50 – 1:20 | 30s)
> *"In our live canonical scenario, North America East revenue suffered a critical -$1.23 million or -7.97% deficit against baseline. Our causal decomposition engine immediately explains 100% of the variance, isolating an Atlanta Distribution Center stockout as the primary driver responsible for 43.2% or -$550,000 of the drop at 94% confidence."*

### Slide 7: Trust & Cryptographic Lineage (1:20 – 1:40 | 20s)
> *"Enterprise decision-makers can trust these findings because every single claim is backed by empirical records with 64-character SHA-256 cryptographic verification hashes. If confidence falls below our 65% mandatory threshold, the system safely abstains from generative reasoning rather than risk a hallucination."*

### Slide 8 & 9: Decision Graph & Simulation (1:40 – 2:20 | 40s)
> *"Through our 6-column Decision Graph, InsightPilot AI maps the Atlanta stockout directly to a Priority 1 action lever: an emergency inter-facility stock transfer of 3,200 units from Chicago, unlocking $484,000 in revenue recovery. In our live simulation sandbox, raising inventory availability from 79.4% to 90.0% models a +$341,422 recovery and a 1.4-point gross margin lift."*

### Slide 10, 11 & 12: Business Impact & Closing (2:20 – 3:00 | 40s)
> *"InsightPilot AI reduces triage time by ~95%, identifies over $757,000 in immediate recovery opportunities, and completely eliminates hallucinations by architectural design. Enterprise intelligence should not stop at detecting what changed—it must explain why, prove it with evidence, and simulate what to do next. InsightPilot AI turns data into decisions. Thank you."*
