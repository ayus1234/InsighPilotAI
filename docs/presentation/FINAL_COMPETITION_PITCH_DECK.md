## 5. Judge Q&A Playbook

### Technical & Architecture Questions

**Q1: Why did you choose LangGraph instead of a simple single-prompt LLM chain?**  
*Answer:* Complex enterprise investigations require cyclical state management, safety gates, and deterministic validation between stages. LangGraph provides an explicit 11-node state graph where each step—such as SHA-256 validation or confidence scoring—can deterministically control execution flow and safely trigger fallback or abstention before any generative model is called.

**Q2: How do you handle multi-provider AI failover?**  
*Answer:* Our router implements sequential failover across Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) and Google Gemini (Gemini 2.5 Flash). If external APIs encounter rate limits or timeouts, the system automatically falls back to deterministic grounded templates, ensuring zero system downtime during executive presentations.

---

### AI Safety & Trust Questions

**Q3: How does InsightPilot AI guarantee zero hallucinations in executive reporting?**  
*Answer:* By strict architectural design: the LLM never calculates numbers. Quantitative facts are computed by Python analytics and injected into structured prompt schemas. After generation, an automated grounding validator checks that every cited fact and entity matches verified evidence IDs. If an unverified claim is detected, it is immediately flagged and rejected.

**Q4: What is the purpose of the 65% Mandatory Abstention Threshold?**  
*Answer:* In enterprise decision-making, an ungrounded or low-confidence hallucination is far more dangerous than acknowledging uncertainty. When conflicting signals or missing data cause our 6-factor confidence score to fall below 65%, InsightPilot AI deliberately abstains from generative reasoning and delivers transparent, responsible uncertainty messaging.
