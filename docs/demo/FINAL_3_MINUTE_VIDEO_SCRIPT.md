## 🎬 Recording & Presentation Pro-Tips

1. **Resolution & Scaling:** Record at `1920x1080` (1080p Full HD) at 100% browser zoom with the browser maximized.
2. **Smooth Cursor Work:** Move your mouse with deliberate, smooth arcs. Avoid rapid shaking or jittery scrolling.
3. **Pacing:** Speak with a calm, executive cadence (~130–140 words per minute).
4. **File Format & Compression:** Export as `.mp4` (H.264 video codec, AAC audio codec). Ensure the final file size is strictly $\le$ 20 MB for seamless competition portal upload.
